Why Nature Matters

Nature provides us with clean air, fresh water, food, and beautiful places to explore. Spending time in nature can also help us relax and stay healthy.

Benefits of Spending Time in Nature

  • Reduces stress and helps us relax
  • Improves physical and mental well-being
  • Provides opportunities for outdoor activities
  • Helps us appreciate and protect our environment
